Performance Analysis of Dynamic Routing Protocols (IPv4 & IPv6)

Networking Research Published via IRJET

This research paper presents a comprehensive analysis of dynamic routing protocols including OSPF, EIGRP, and IS-IS across both IPv4 and IPv6 network environments. The study evaluates critical performance metrics such as convergence time, packet loss, throughput, and latency to determine optimal routing strategies for modern network infrastructures.

The findings demonstrate significant performance improvements through strategic protocol redistribution and provide actionable insights for network engineers designing resilient, high-availability networks.
