CO₂ Emissions Predictive Modeling (SEMMA Methodology)

Predictive Analytics & Machine Learning

Graduate Team Project · UConn School of Business

CHALLENGE

Predict log-transformed CO₂ emissions across 20 years of global energy transition metrics; led 3-person preprocessing team for missing-value treatment, outlier handling, and PCA on a 60/20/20 split.

SOLUTION

Built and benchmarked 13 machine learning models including 5 linear regression variants, decision tree, random forest, boosted tree, KNN, neural network, and 3 ensembles under SEMMA framework.

RESULTS

Identified tuned KNN (k=2) as top performer with tuned boosted tree as runner-up; reporting results in log and original kt scales with a one-page executive summary for non-technical leadership.
